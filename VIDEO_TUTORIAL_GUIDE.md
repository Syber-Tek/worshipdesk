### Step-by-Step Production Script & Actions (Video 1)

#### `00:00` – Introduction
- **What to Record:**
  - Screen capture of WorshipDesk opening to the clean Dashboard.
  - Quick cuts showing the bilingual scripture projection and hymn projection in action.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Welcome to WorshipDesk! In this video, you'll learn everything you need to run a smooth Sunday church service from the projection desk. WorshipDesk is built from the ground up to be 100% offline, meaning no awkward Wi-Fi drops mid-service. Whether your church projects in English, Twi, Ewe, or both side-by-side, this guide will get you confident in less than 15 minutes. Let’s jump right in!"*

---

#### `01:15` – Quick Interface Tour
- **What to Record:**
  - Cursor pointing to the left navigation bar (Dashboard, Scripture, Hymns, Service Planner, Settings).
  - Cursor moving to the right **Live Rail**:
    - Highlight **Live Output** (what the congregation sees right now).
    - Highlight **Next Staged** (what is lined up next).
    - Highlight the transport buttons: **Present**, **Next**, **Previous**, **Clear**.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"When you open WorshipDesk, you’ll notice a 3-part layout designed for quick control. On the left, you have your main tabs: Dashboard, Scripture, Hymns, and Service Planner. On the far right is your Live Rail. Think of this like your broadcast deck: 'Live' shows exactly what is projected on the church screens, and 'Next' shows what is queued up. The controls at the bottom let you present, advance, or immediately clear the screen."*

---

#### `02:45` – Presenting Scriptures
- **What to Record:**
  - Click on the **Scripture** tab.
  - Type `John 3:16` in the search box; press Enter or click search.
  - Show the verse appearing instantly in the list.
  - Click the verse to stage it in **Next**, then click **Present** (or press Spacebar).
  - Show the live output window updating to display the verse.
  - Press the **Next Arrow** to move to verse 17.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Let's project our first scripture. Click on the Scripture tab. You can search by reference like 'John 3:16' or search for keywords like 'shepherd' or 'grace'. Click on the verse to stage it, and hit Present. The verse is instantly projected on your church screens. Use your right arrow key to advance through the passage verse-by-verse as the pastor reads."*

---

#### `04:15` – Bilingual Scripture Dual-View (English + Twi / Ewe)
- **What to Record:**
  - In the Scripture tab, turn on the **Dual Translation** toggle.
  - Select **Primary:** `NIV` (English).
  - Select **Parallel:** `Twi Asante` (or `Ewe`).
  - Search `Psalm 23:1`.
  - Point out how `Psalms` automatically locks to `Nnwom` (canonical book lock).
  - Click **Present** and show the split screen output on the projector.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Here is one of WorshipDesk’s most powerful features: Parallel Dual-View. Many of our church services are bilingual. When you enable Dual Translation, you can pair English NIV with Twi Asante or Ewe. Notice that WorshipDesk locks books canonically—so English 'Psalms' automatically pairs with Twi 'Nnwom', and 'John' pairs with 'Yohane'. When you hit Present, the projector splits cleanly into a side-by-side view with formatted references for both languages."*

---

#### `05:45` – Presenting Hymns & Stanza Navigation
- **What to Record:**
  - Click the **Hymns** tab.
  - Select category (e.g., `Presby Hymns - Eng` or `Methodist Hymns - Twi`).
  - Search by hymn number: `120`.
  - Open the hymn and point to the **Stanza Chips** (`Stanza 1 / 5`, `Stanza 2 / 5`, `Chorus`).
  - Click **Stanza 1** and press **Present**.
  - Click the **Chorus** chip directly to jump out of order as the choir leads.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Next up: Hymns and praise lyrics. Go to the Hymns tab. You can browse categories like Presbyterian or Methodist Hymnals in English and Twi. If the pastor announces 'Hymn 120', just type 120 and press Enter. Each hymn is automatically divided into clean stanza slides. You can advance stanza-by-stanza, or click any chip directly if the song leader skips straight to the chorus."*

---

#### `07:15` – Media Slides & Motion Backgrounds
- **What to Record:**
  - Show how to add a full-screen media slide (Image, Video, or PDF).
  - Demonstrate the fit toggles: **Show Whole** (letterboxed) vs. **Fill Screen** (full bleed).
  - Demonstrate a sermon PDF: load a 3-page PDF slide and use Next/Prev to flip pages.
  - Show video playback: click **Replay** and toggle **Mute/Unmute**.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Media isn't a separate app here—it lives directly in your service plan. You can project church announcements, welcome videos, and pastor's PDF slides. You have full control over fit: 'Show Whole' prevents cropping, while 'Fill Screen' gives you edge-to-edge visuals. For video announcements, you have independent mute controls and a one-click Replay button."*

---

#### `08:45` – Building the Sunday Service Plan
- **What to Record:**
  - Click **Service Planner**.
  - Assemble a quick Sunday routine:
    1. Welcome Image
    2. Opening Hymn (Hymn 12)
    3. Scripture Reading (Dual-view John 14:1-6)
    4. Sermon PDF
    5. Closing Hymn
  - Drag an item up and down to change order.
  - Show **Edit in Place**: click to edit an announcement text directly without rebuilding the slide.
  - Click **Save Plan** (`SundayService.worship`).
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Instead of scrambling during the service, prepare your entire Order of Service ahead of time in the Service Planner. Add hymns, scriptures, and media. Reorder them using drag-and-drop, and edit any slide text directly in place. Once ready, save your plan as a .worship file. On Sunday morning, open your file and simply press Next to step through the entire service seamlessly."*

---

#### `10:15` – Emergency Controls & Pro Tips
- **What to Record:**
  - Demonstrate clicking the **Clear** button (screen blanks while maintaining position).
  - Show keyboard hotkeys: `Right Arrow` for Next, `Left Arrow` for Previous, `F5` or `Spacebar` to Present.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Here are two golden rules for Sunday operators: First, if the speaker goes off-script or makes a sudden altar call, click the 'Clear' button. This blanks the projector instantly without closing any windows. Second, learn your hotkeys: Spacebar presents, and your arrow keys step through slides, keeping your hands off the mouse so you can follow the service closely."*

---

#### `11:15` – Wrap-Up & Outro
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"And that is how you operate a Sunday service using WorshipDesk! In our next video, we’ll dive deep into Settings: how to configure dual monitors, set up the stage confidence display for the choir, import custom church hymn files, and add new Bible versions. Leave a comment below if you have any questions, and don't forget to like and subscribe!"*

#### `00:00` – Introduction

---

  - Click **Settings → Display & Projector**.
  - Show the **Control Display** (Monitor 1) vs **Projection Output** (Monitor 2).

#### `02:45` – Stage Confidence Monitor Setup
- **What to Record:**
  - In Display settings, show the **Stage Display** section.
  - Toggle **Stage Confidence Monitor** to ON.
  - Show the Stage Display window:
    - Large high-contrast text.
    - Live digital clock (`HH:MM:SS AM/PM`).
    - Next slide preview banner.

  > *"Right below that is the Stage Display toggle. This launches a dedicated 3rd screen view for your pastors, worship team, and choir. #### `04:15` – Presentation Styling (Themes, Fonts & Margins)
- **What to Record:**
  - Click **Settings → Presentation**.
  - Show the background theme selector:
    - **Obsidian Dark**
    - **Light Clean**
    - **Custom Image** (click and select a wallpaper)
    - **Motion Video** (select bundled loops like *Aurora Lights* or upload a custom `.mp4`)

  - Adjust the **Slide Margins** (padding).
  - Adjust **Attribution Position** (Bottom, Top, Bottom-Right).
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Under the Presentation tab, you control how your slides look. You can switch between Dark Obsidian, Daylight Light, or select a dynamic Motion Video loop like Aurora Lights or Galaxy Stars. You can also upload your church’s branded background image or video loop. Use the font sliders to dial in the perfect text scale for your projector’s throw distance, and set where scripture references appear—top, bottom, or bottom-right."*

---

#### `06:00` – Bible Settings & Uploading Custom Bibles
- **What to Record:**
  - Click **Settings → Bible & Scripture**.
  - Change **Default Bible Translation** (e.g., set to NIV or NKJV).
  - Show the **Import Bible SQL File (.sql)** button.
  - Show the **Import Bible XML File (.xml)** button (mention OSIS & Zefania XML formats).
  - Highlight the info box displaying where Bibles live on disk:
    - Windows: `%APPDATA%\WorshipDesk\bibles\` or `bibles/xml/`
  - Show the **Installed Bible Library** stats list.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Under Bible & Scripture, you set your church's default translation on startup. But what if you want to add a translation that isn't pre-loaded? You have two easy options: First, you can click 'Select SQL File' or 'Select XML File' to import Zefania or OSIS formatted Bibles directly through the interface. Second, you can simply drop Bible XML files into your app data folder under bibles/xml. WorshipDesk scans and seeds them into the offline SQLite database automatically."*

---

#### `08:15` – Songs & Hymn Preferences
- **What to Record:**

  - Change **Default Hymnal Category** (e.g., Presby Hymns - Eng).
  - Toggle **Show Hymn Numbers** on and off (demonstrate how "PH 120" appears on projector).
  - Adjust the **Projector Hymn Text Scale** slider.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"Under Songs & Hymns, you can set the default hymnal category that opens when your team clicks the Hymns tab. You can toggle hymn number labels on or off—so whether your congregation needs to see 'PH 45' or just the clean lyrics, you decide. You also have a dedicated text scaling slider specifically for hymn lyrics."*

---

#### `09:45` – Importing Custom Hymn Batches
- **What to Record:**
  - Under Songs settings or Content settings, click **Import Hymns JSON Batch**.
  - Show the file dialog selecting a sample songs JSON file.
  - Show the success toast: *"Imported X hymns!"*.
  - Explain folder-based song additions in `%APPDATA%\WorshipDesk\hymns\<CategoryName>\`.
- **Voiceover / What to Say:**
  > *"To import custom church hymns or choir anthems in bulk, click 'Select JSON File' under Import Hymns JSON Batch. WorshipDesk will parse the verses and insert them directly into your offline database. You can also organize song text files into subfolders in your AppData directory—each subfolder automatically becomes a selectable category in the app!"*

---

#### `11:15` – Content & Database Backup

  - Click **Settings → Content & Backup**.
  - Show the **SQLite Database Connection** status (`Connected - WAL Mode`).

Before making major updates or moving to a new church laptop, click 'Backup Local Database'. ## 🛠️ Recording Setup & Editing Checklist

1. **Resolution & Scaling:**
   - Record at `1920x1080` or `2560x1440`.
   - In Windows Display Settings, keep display scaling at `100%` or `125%` so text remains crisp and readable.
2. **OBS Scene Layout Recommendation:**
   - **Scene 1 (Full Operator):** Main operator window taking 100% of the screen.
   - **Scene 2 (Picture-in-Picture):** Main operator window with a small window in the bottom-right showing the live projector output.
   - **Scene 3 (Split 50/50):** Operator screen on the left, Projector output on the right (ideal for demonstrating bilingual scripture pairing).
3. **Pacing & Cursors:**
   - Use high-visibility cursor highlights (e.g., yellow circle).
   - Pause for 1 second after clicking buttons before speaking, giving viewers time to register the UI change.
