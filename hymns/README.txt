Church Presenter — Presby Hymns & Songs Importer Folder
===========================================================
Place your Presbyterian Hymns (.sng or .txt files) in this folder to auto-import them:

1. Worship Live / Song Files (.sng):
   - Drop your English and Twi .sng files directly here or in subfolders (e.g. hymns/Presby/).

2. JSON Song Collections (.json):
   - Drop any JSON song collections here.

The application automatically scans and imports all hymns into SQLite on startup!
